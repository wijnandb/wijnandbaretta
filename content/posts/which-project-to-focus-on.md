Whatever I decide to do, which project to focus on, there are some questions I need to ask before I start working on any of them. These questions are:
- Why?
What is the reason you want to work on this project? Are you solving a problem, is it for fun, do you see a huge opportunity, is it simply something you are ghood at doing, WHY do you want to work on this project?

- What is "the job to be done"?
The late Clayton Christensen wrote about this. Determining what the "job to be done" is, gives you a clearer perspective on what you are actually trying to achieve. Without looking at the job to be done, you might be veering off in the wrong direction. Think milkshakes for commuters and milkshakes for kids.

- Where is the money?
Or: who is the client, what is the business model. Stop doing things without considering the business model or explicitly decide that this project is purely for fun and tell your loved ones that as well...

Optional questions include: 
- What are the alternatives?
