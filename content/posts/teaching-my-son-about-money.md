# Teaching My Son About Money

*This is a draft. Write freely. Edit later. Publish when ready.*
I have great kids. A bit spoilt, but really nice. Happy, enjoying life, what more can you wish for? Not a lot, I admit. I am very glad and happy that they are doing fine, so no complaints here. Not about dropping out of university or excessive drinking, I really believe it is all about growing up. 

And yet...
I have a rsponsibility as a father. 
So I keep telling them they need to make sure they earn enough money to do the things they want to do.
And somehow, they don't succeed. There's always some days left when the money runs out.
Or more accurately: we frequently get a phonecall (hurray! They know how to use their addiction device to contact us!) asking us urgently to please transfer some money. Which we then do, yeah, most of the time. Suckers. 

Before transferiing the money, we have the talk: 
- didn't you see this coming? 
- You are not working enough.
- if you spend more than you earn, you'll run out of money. Either earn more or spend less.

Obviously it doesn't work.
So, that got me thinking: what will work?

And I thought the best way to deal with this, is really showing them what's going on. Let them understand how their personal finance works, day by day, week by week. Work now, and you'll get some money in a month time. But when it arrives, you'll have spend a lot, resulting in a biggr deficit than you have now. So it probably won't be enough.

So the idea was, to make them see in to the future.
