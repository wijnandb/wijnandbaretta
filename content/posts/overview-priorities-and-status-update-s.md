So let's go over everything that's keeping me/my mind busy:

### Brede School

On-going, main things to deal with are:
  - get paid for the hours you put in
  - be smart about the way you handle the project (organizing the work vs. doing the actual programming work)
  
Taking these two conclusions, the way forward is:
- ask for the amount of money available
- clarify that this isn't about *getting the project finished*, but more about "what do you want to improve". 
- get a team of people that are working with this software and discuss the various aspects
- determine what needs to be done first
- specify everything
- hire someone to build it

This boils down to writing a plan 2022. Don't think it has to be very elaborate, it can be done with bullet-points and an appoitment to do a presentation. You talk easier than you write, which does NOT mean you don't write well.

### Get your personal site up
Why are you postponing this?

Scared?

That's okay, but think about the possible downside. What is it? What can it be? Have faith in yourself and the things you care about. The advantage is that at the moment, there are more jobs than there are people to fill those jobs. It's okay if you don't know what you'll end up doing, go ahead and start figuring it out. By making yourself visible, you also have a chance of *being found*. It will show who you are and what you can do. Go for it, you deserve it!

By creating a (or even more than one) nice starting-page, which contains relevant keywords, which link through to pages with articles about those keywords, you'll create a blend of resume/portfolio which can help you significantly to get a job.
