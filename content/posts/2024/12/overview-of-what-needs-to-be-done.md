What I am after, is a way to easily get back into what I was doing, what I need to do, what I was working on.
And I have already made steps to achieve that, by using ToDoist, implementing the [Eisenhower matrix](eisenhower-matrix.html) and setting up this website.

However, I still want more and better.

The pages with projects are crappy and I could also improve on working on my ToDoist lists, currently I am not doing my weekly review nor am I really using it as a planning tool.

So, what could be improved?
Sometimes I think what I really love is creating something new, not necessarily because it will work better but because it is new.

One of the ideas in my head is that I would like to go to a page, where I have all my projects listed.

I can click on a project and then see sub-projects and their todo items. Basically that is what ToDoist is, right?
So why am I unhappy about the way I am workinhg with ToDoist?
It's because I don't clean up.

If I would take it more seriously as a planning and productivity tool, I would do the weekly review and I would remove items that are not needed anymore.

It would still be nice to have something like a roadmap, maybe OKR's, an Eisenhower matrix filled by ToDOist items,

Well, that can be created.

I already have a ChatGPT which [explains how I can take ToDoist items and plot them on an Eisenhower matrix](https://chatgpt.com/c/6755d9b8-4cd4-800a-be3b-15e0350b5730).

Now I'd love to go and create this, but I have to fair to myself: is this directly contributing to my goals? 
Is this pushing any of my OKR's forward?

The honest answer is no.

So I either have to find another reason why I can work on this ("create a post showing how to improve productivity by utilising AI", or another one: "Software development with ChatGPT"), or I have to postpone it and work on more pressing issues first.

OK, I have been quite productive today, so it feels as if I deserve to do something fun like the project mentioned above.
