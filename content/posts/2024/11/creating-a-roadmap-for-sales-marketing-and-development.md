By creating a roadmap for IkTel and break down the work to be done by area (Sales, Marketing and Development), I hope to achieve more focus and productivity.

Obviously, I asked the help of ChatGPT. This was their response:

# Ideal Future Plan for the After-School Activities Platform

## **A Year from Now: The Ideal Situation**

### **What Does the Business Look Like?**
1. **Platform Adoption and Scale**  
   - The platform is widely recognized and used by schools across several regions in the Netherlands.  
   - Schools, school boards, and activity providers actively recommend the platform.  
   - The platform serves a growing user base, with regular improvements driven by feedback.

2. **Efficient Operations**  
   - Schools and organizations have reduced their administrative burdens, leading to better-organized, high-quality after-school programs.  
   - Parents and children experience seamless enrollment and participation.

3. **Strong Brand Presence**  
   - The platform is a trusted brand in education and after-school programming.  
   - Your website, https://iktel.nl/, ranks high in search results and clearly communicates the value proposition.

4. **Solid Business Foundation**  
   - Reliable revenue streams, including subscriptions or licensing from schools and organizations.  
   - Positive case studies and testimonials drive confidence among potential customers.

5. **Organized Internal Structure**  
   - Teams in place for marketing, sales, development, onboarding, helpdesk, and CRM, ensuring all aspects of the business run smoothly.

---

## **Steps to Achieve the Ideal Situation**

### **1. Marketing**
- **Goal**: Build awareness and trust in the education sector.
- **Actions**:  
  - Launch targeted campaigns aimed at schools and school boards, showcasing the platform's success stories (e.g., case studies from Amsterdam Zuidoost).  
  - Collaborate with influencers or thought leaders in education.  
  - Publish regular blog posts and whitepapers about the importance of after-school activities and how your platform makes them accessible.  
  - Attend and sponsor educational conferences or events.  
- **Needed**:  
  - A content calendar with planned blog posts, social media campaigns, and newsletters.  
  - Marketing automation tools to streamline campaigns.  
  - A budget for ads and partnerships.

### **2. Sales**
- **Goal**: Create and close deals with new schools and school boards.
- **Actions**:  
  - Develop a sales playbook: outline the pitch, common objections, and benefits.  
  - Set up a demo team to give schools hands-on experience with the platform.  
  - Offer pilot programs to schools hesitant about full adoption.  
  - Build relationships with local and national education bodies to identify potential clients.  
- **Needed**:  
  - A CRM system (e.g., HubSpot or Salesforce) to track leads and manage the sales pipeline.  
  - A dedicated sales team or point person for outreach.

### **3. Development**
- **Goal**: Ensure the platform is robust, scalable, and user-friendly.
- **Actions**:  
  - Implement continuous feedback loops to capture user suggestions and bugs.  
  - Prioritize features that support scale (e.g., multilingual support, improved matching algorithms).  
  - Build a mobile-friendly interface to make access easier for parents and teachers.  
- **Needed**:  
  - A roadmap for development milestones, reviewed quarterly.  
  - A QA/testing process to ensure reliability.  
  - Adequate hosting and scaling infrastructure.

### **4. Onboarding**
- **Goal**: Make it easy for schools, companies, and parents to start using the platform.
- **Actions**:  
  - Create step-by-step guides and videos tailored for each user type.  
  - Assign onboarding managers to guide schools during the initial setup phase.  
  - Implement an in-app onboarding experience to walk users through key features.  
- **Needed**:  
  - User-friendly documentation.  
  - A team or tool for personalized onboarding sessions.  
  - Feedback collection post-onboarding to continuously improve.

### **5. Helpdesk**
- **Goal**: Provide timely and effective support to all users.
- **Actions**:  
  - Set up a centralized helpdesk system (e.g., Zendesk or Freshdesk) to handle support tickets.  
  - Develop an FAQ page addressing common concerns for schools, parents, and activity providers.  
  - Train a small team to handle support during key operational hours.  
- **Needed**:  
  - A knowledge base for internal and external support documentation.  
  - Helpdesk software for ticketing, chat, and phone support.  
  - Regular training for helpdesk staff on updates and user pain points.

### **6. CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**
- **Goal**: Keep users engaged and informed while tracking their journey.
- **Actions**:  
  - Use CRM tools to manage relationships, track satisfaction, and analyze usage trends.  
  - Automate follow-ups, such as reminders for upcoming program enrollments.  
  - Segment your audience to deliver personalized communications (e.g., schools vs. parents).  
- **Needed**:  
  - A robust CRM tool (same as for sales, if possible) that integrates with your platform.  
  - A plan for ongoing engagement through emails, reports, and platform updates.

---

## **What Do You Need to Get There?**

### **People**
- **Marketing**: A digital marketer and a content creator.  
- **Sales**: A sales manager or team member experienced in education technology.  
- **Development**: Additional developers to scale the platform and ensure reliability.  
- **Onboarding**: A customer success manager to handle onboarding and feedback.  
- **Helpdesk**: Support staff (start with one or two people).  
- **CRM**: A data analyst or CRM specialist to manage customer interactions and insights.

### **Tools**
- Marketing: Social media management tools (e.g., Buffer), analytics (Google Analytics), and design tools (Canva or Adobe).  
- CRM and sales: HubSpot or Salesforce.  
- Helpdesk: Zendesk, Freshdesk, or an equivalent.  
- Development: Project management tools like Jira or Trello.

### **Budget**
- Allocate resources for:  
  - Marketing campaigns and conferences.  
  - CRM and helpdesk tools.  
  - Hiring key team members.  
  - Hosting and scaling infrastructure.

### **Milestones**
- **Quarter 1**: Finalize your marketing and sales strategy. Secure early adopters.  
- **Quarter 2**: Launch targeted marketing campaigns. Expand onboarding support.  
- **Quarter 3**: Scale helpdesk operations and launch new platform features.  
- **Quarter 4**: Evaluate feedback and plan for further expansion.

---

With a clear vision, defined steps, and the right resources, you'll be on track to achieve a thriving, scalable business that supports schools and enhances after-school programs.
