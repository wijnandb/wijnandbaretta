Friday. Rainy. Dark. November. 

I have a meeting/presentation on Monday and I'm dreading it. It could be procrastination, it could be fear, I don't know. Maybe  I have been out of it too long (I wanted to write "out of the game", but it sounded stupid and pretentious. That's also a thing: I tend to be very critically towards myself. A terrible fear or reluctnace to express myself).

So, why am I scared or procrastinating or whatever this is?

I think I'm good at this, I can build a really good platform to organize after-school activities. And yet, it isn't really there yet. 

So why is that?
You have been building yourself, which is also something you like. You have also hired someone to build for you, which makes sense because there is too much to do. Even with this guy onboard, you are still not making the progress you want. Why is that?

You are not overly satisfied with the quality/work ethic of your developer. 
You are also not sure about your own way of working, you could be a bit more directive.

Let's send this guy a message, tell him you are unhappy.

---
Update:
Instead of telling him I was unhappy, I created a document outlining what I was unhappy about.
Then after writing it down, I decided to *promote* him, ask him to be the CTO.
Why?
I don't really know exactly, but I guess I needed to step out of the role of CTO, so I could have time to focus on sales and marketing.
And while we don't know yet if appointing this developer as CTO will turn out to be a success, I am already feeling liberated. 

So: let's get cranking on sales and marketing!
