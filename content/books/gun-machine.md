# Gun Machine by Warren Ellis

## Summary

"Gun Machine" is a fast-paced noir detective novel by Warren Ellis. The story centers around NYPD Detective John Tallow, who finds himself thrust into a bizarre and sprawling case after his partner is killed. In the same incident that claims his partner’s life, Tallow discovers an apartment full of guns, seemingly unconnected but ultimately tied to a string of unsolved murders stretching back over a decade.

As the investigation unfolds, Tallow is met with various obstacles, including city politics and corruption. The book delves into themes of isolation, urban life, and the psychological impacts of working in law enforcement. The narrative is peppered with Ellis's characteristic dark humor and intricate plotting.

## Review

"Warren Ellis's 'Gun Machine' has been praised for its gripping narrative and distinct voice. The novel combines a traditional detective story with elements of a psychological thriller, creating a compelling mystery filled with rich detail and atmosphere. 

Ellis's strength lies in his ability to weave a complex plot with vivid settings and eccentric characters. However, some critics mention that while the characterization is strong, the pacing can occasionally stall with less focus on building emotional depth.

The book has been lauded for its ability to balance suspense with intelligent commentary on modern urban existence, making it a satisfying read for fans of the crime genre."

## Key Takeaways

- **Intricate Plotting**: The novel shows how seemingly unrelated elements can weave together to create a complex story.
- **Impact of Setting**: Ellis uses New York City as more than just a backdrop, showing how the environment shapes the narrative.
- **Themes of Isolation and Society**: Explores how isolation can affect individuals, especially in urban settings.
- **Dark Humor**: The use of humor in dark situations can provide relief and deeper understanding of characters.

## Recommendation

"Gun Machine" is highly recommended for readers who enjoy tightly-plotted detective stories and urban noir. Fans of Warren Ellis's previous works or those who appreciate gritty narratives with a psychological edge will find this novel particularly engaging. It's well-suited for anyone interested in exploring the darker aspects of humanity through the lens of modern crime fiction.
