# What Every BODY is Saying: An Ex-FBI Agent’s Guide to Speed-Reading People

## Summary
"What Every BODY is Saying" is a guide to understanding nonverbal communication by Joe Navarro, a former FBI counterintelligence officer, with contributions from Marvin Karlins. The book is structured around introducing readers to the fundamental role that body language plays in conveying emotions and intentions. Navarro delves into various involuntary physical cues ranging from facial expressions to foot placement, interpreting how these can indicate underlying thoughts and feelings. The book details the importance of context in understanding nonverbal signals and stresses situational awareness.

## Review
The book has been well-received for its pragmatic approach to decoding body language, backed by Joe Navarro’s extensive experience in the FBI. Readers find the book informative and easy to understand, appreciating its clear explanations and illustrative examples. A notable strength is Navarro’s methodical breakdown of complex psychological concepts into accessible segments. A critique, however, is that the content may sometimes rely heavily on anecdotes without offering scientific research to bolster claims, potentially limiting its academic rigor. Nonetheless, its practical advice and real-world applications make it valuable for laypeople interested in enhancing their interpersonal skills.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Nonverbal Communication is Crucial**: Understanding body language can significantly enhance interpersonal communication and awareness.
2. **Baselining Behavior**: Establishing a baseline for individual behavior is essential for accurately interpreting deviations that suggest deception or discomfort.
3. **Significance of Context**: Interpretation of nonverbal cues must always take into account the context to avoid misinterpretations.
4. **Focus on the Whole Picture**: Effective reading of body language involves observing clusters of nonverbal signals rather than isolated gestures.

## Recommendation
"What Every BODY is Saying" is highly recommended for anyone interested in mastering the art of nonverbal communication, from professionals in law enforcement to anyone looking to improve personal relationships. The book provides actionable insights that can be beneficial in various settings — from negotiations to social gatherings — helping readers better understand and respond to unspoken messages.
