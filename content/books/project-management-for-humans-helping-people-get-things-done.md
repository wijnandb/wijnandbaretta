# Project Management for Humans: Helping People Get Things Done by Brett Harned

## Summary
"Project Management for Humans" by Brett Harned offers a comprehensive introduction to the principles of project management, tailored to be accessible for individuals both inside and outside of the field. The book emphasizes practical, real-world applications of project management techniques to help readers effectively organize their work and coordinate with teams. Harned’s approach is grounded in the belief that successful project management involves fostering human connections and enhancing communication among team members. The text is structured to guide readers through the process of initiating, executing, and completing projects with an emphasis on collaboration and adaptability.

## Review
The book has been well-received for its approachable writing style and practical advice, making it a valuable resource for beginners and seasoned professionals alike. Harned draws from his extensive experience in the industry to share insights and strategies that are applicable across various types of projects. A strength of the book is its focus on the human element of project management, which is often overlooked in more technical manuals. Some readers might find the lack of depth in certain technical aspects a drawback if they are looking for in-depth technical guidance.

## Key Takeaways
- **Emphasize Communication**: Effective project management relies heavily on strong communication skills to ensure that everyone on the team is aligned and informed.
- **Adaptability is Key**: Being flexible and open to change is crucial, as projects rarely go exactly as planned.
- **Focus on Relationships**: Building and maintaining strong interpersonal relationships helps in resolving conflicts and ensuring project success.
- **Practical Tools and Techniques**: Utilize straightforward tools and techniques for planning, executing, and monitoring projects.
- **Human-Centric Approach**: Understand and leverage human behavior to motivate teams and drive projects to completion.

## Recommendation
This book is recommended for professionals new to project management, team leaders, or anyone interested in enhancing their ability to manage tasks and people. Its human-centric approach makes it an excellent choice for those who believe in the importance of communication and relationship-building within the workplace. The book's practical advice and accessible language make it suitable for a broad audience, including those who do not have a formal background in project management.
