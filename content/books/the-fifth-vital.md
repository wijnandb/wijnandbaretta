# **The Fifth Vital** by Mike Majlak and Riley J. Ford

## **Summary**
"The Fifth Vital" is a candid memoir by Mike Majlak, co-authored with Riley J. Ford, detailing Majlak's tumultuous journey through opioid addiction and his subsequent path to recovery and success. The title refers to the concept of pain as the "fifth vital sign," highlighting the pervasive issue of pain management in the opioid crisis. Majlak shares his experiences with raw honesty, offering insights into the struggles of addiction and the resilience required to overcome it. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## **Chapters**

### **1. The Beginning**
Introduces Mike's early life in Milford, Connecticut, and his initial exposure to drugs during his teenage years. Sets the stage for his descent into addiction.

### **2. The Descent**
Chronicles the escalation of Mike's drug use, leading to a full-blown opioid addiction. Describes the impact on his relationships, health, and daily life.

### **3. Rock Bottom**
Details the lowest points of Mike's addiction, including legal troubles, loss of trust from loved ones, and near-death experiences.

### **4. The Turning Point**
Explores the moments and influences that motivated Mike to seek help and begin his journey toward recovery.

### **5. The Road to Recovery**
Discusses the challenges and triumphs Mike faced during rehabilitation, including relapses and the strategies he employed to maintain sobriety.

### **6. Rebuilding Life**
Focuses on Mike's efforts to rebuild his personal and professional life post-recovery, including reconnecting with family and pursuing new career opportunities.

### **7. Finding Purpose**
Highlights how Mike discovered a sense of purpose through sharing his story, engaging in public speaking, and becoming a social media personality.

### **8. The Power of Influence**
Examines Mike's rise as a social media influencer, co-hosting the "Impaulsive" podcast, and using his platform to raise awareness about addiction and recovery.

### **9. Reflections**
Offers Mike's reflections on his journey, the lessons learned, and his perspectives on addiction, recovery, and personal growth.

### **10. Moving Forward**
Concludes with Mike's vision for the future, his ongoing commitment to sobriety, and his aspirations to continue helping others facing similar struggles.

## **Key Takeaways**
- **Resilience**: Overcoming addiction requires immense strength and determination.
- **Support Systems**: The role of family, friends, and community is crucial in the recovery process.
- **Purpose and Passion**: Finding a meaningful purpose can be a powerful motivator in maintaining sobriety.
- **Advocacy**: Sharing personal experiences can inspire and support others facing similar challenges.

---

## **Review**
"The Fifth Vital" is a compelling and unfiltered account of Mike Majlak's battle with opioid addiction and his journey to recovery. The memoir provides valuable insights into the complexities of addiction and the resilience required to overcome it. Majlak's storytelling is both engaging and inspiring, making this book a significant contribution to discussions on addiction and recovery. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
