# You Took the Last Bus Home: The Poems of Brian Bilston

## Summary
"You Took the Last Bus Home" is a collection of poetry by Brian Bilston, often regarded as the "poet laureate of Twitter." Known for his wit, humor, and relatable observations on modern life, Bilston's poetry tackles everyday topics with a clever twist. The collection includes poems that explore themes such as love, technology, politics, and social media, often with a humorous or satirical edge. His work is characterized by clever wordplay, unexpected punchlines, and a style that is accessible to a broad audience.

## Review
Brian Bilston's poetry collection has been praised for its accessibility and humor. Many readers appreciate the blend of clever wordplay with poignant observations on contemporary life. The book effectively captures the spirit of modern communication and social commentary, resonating with those who enjoy light-hearted yet insightful poetry. Critics sometimes point out that the simplicity and humor can occasionally overshadow deeper themes. Nevertheless, Bilston's style is generally seen as refreshing and engaging.

## Key Takeaways
- Everyday life can be a rich source of poetic inspiration, especially when viewed through a humorous lens.
- Technology and social media are integral parts of modern life, and their impact can be both amusing and thought-provoking.
- Clever wordplay and humor can make poetry more accessible and relatable to a wider audience.
- Humorous poetry can still offer insightful commentary on serious topics such as politics and societal norms.

## Recommendation
"You Took the Last Bus Home" is recommended for readers who enjoy whimsical and humorous poetry with a contemporary twist. It's suitable for those looking for an entertaining read that also engages with modern societal themes. Fans of social media and witty observations on everyday life will find this collection particularly appealing.
