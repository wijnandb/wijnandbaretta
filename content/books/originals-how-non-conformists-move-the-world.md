# Originals: How Non-Conformists Move the World by Adam Grant

## Summary
"Originals: How Non-Conformists Move the World" by Adam Grant explores the idea of originality and the role non-conformists play in driving change in society. The book delves into how individuals can champion new ideas and resist conformity despite the inherent risks. Grant examines stories from a variety of fields such as business, politics, and entertainment to illustrate how originality can be cultivated and embraced. He focuses on the balance between creative thinking and practical execution, offering insights into overcoming the fear of failure and effectively challenging the status quo.

## Review
Adam Grant's "Originals" has been praised for its engaging storytelling and practical insights into fostering originality. The book combines extensive research with compelling anecdotes, making it both informative and entertaining. Critics have highlighted its practical applicability for individuals in creative industries, entrepreneurs, and leaders seeking to inspire innovation. Some have noted the book's broad appeal, though a few critiques mention a reliance on examples that may not be universally relatable. Overall, the book has made a significant impact on readers looking to embrace or encourage innovative thinking.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Procrastination Can Fuel Innovation**: Grant suggests that moderate procrastination can allow ideas to incubate and improve, leading to more creative outcomes.
2. **Embrace a Portfolio of Ideas**: Originals often have a variety of interests and projects, increasing the likelihood of success.
3. **Strategic Risk-Taking**: Balanced risk-taking is emphasized, advocating for a calculated approach rather than reckless boldness.
4. **Question the Default**: The book encourages questioning established norms and default choices in pursuit of originality.
5. **Build a Supportive Network**: Having allies who challenge and support innovative ideas is crucial for successful implementation.
6. **Harnessing Feedback**: Constructive feedback from peers and audiences can refine and enhance original ideas.

## Recommendation
"Originals" is particularly beneficial for entrepreneurs, managers, educators, and creatives who aim to foster innovation and lead change. Individuals looking to understand how to balance creativity with practical execution will find valuable strategies and insights within its pages. The book offers guidance on nurturing originality in oneself and others, making it a useful read for anyone interested in challenging conventions and making an impact.
