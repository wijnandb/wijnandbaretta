# Selfie: How We Became So Self-Obsessed and What It's Doing To Us

## Summary
"Selfie: How We Became So Self-Obsessed and What It's Doing To Us" by Will Storr is a comprehensive exploration of the rise of self-obsession in contemporary society. The book delves into the historical, cultural, and psychological factors that have contributed to the modern focus on the self. Storr traces the origins of self-obsession back to Ancient Greece and follows its evolution through the centuries, highlighting the influence of individualism, neoliberalism, and social media. He examines how the pursuit of self-esteem and perfection affects mental health and well-being, advocating for a more balanced and compassionate understanding of the self.

## Review
Storr's "Selfie" is a well-researched and thought-provoking book that provides a thorough analysis of the causes and effects of self-obsession. The book's strengths lie in its detailed historical context and its interdisciplinary approach, incorporating insights from psychology, sociology, and history. Storr is lauded for his engaging writing style and ability to make complex concepts accessible to a broad audience. However, some critiques mention that the book can be dense at times, with an overwhelming amount of information that may detract from its central arguments.

## Key Takeaways
- The concept of the self has drastically evolved over centuries, influenced by cultural, economic, and technological changes.
- The rise of individualism and neoliberal ideals has amplified the focus on personal success and self-esteem, often leading to self-criticism and societal pressure.
- Social media platforms magnify self-obsession by fostering a culture of comparison and the pursuit of validation through likes and followers.
- Balancing the pursuit of self-improvement with self-compassion is crucial for mental health and fulfillment.

## Recommendation
"Selfie" is recommended for readers interested in psychology, sociology, and cultural studies, particularly those who wish to understand the implications of self-obsession in modern society. It is suitable for anyone interested in exploring the historical causes and contemporary effects of the emphasis on self, as well as for those looking for insights into improving personal well-being in the age of social media.
