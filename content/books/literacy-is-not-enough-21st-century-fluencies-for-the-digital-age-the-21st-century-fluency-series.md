# Literacy Is NOT Enough: 21st Century Fluencies for the Digital Age

## Summary
"Literacy Is NOT Enough" by Andrew Churches, Lee Crockett, and Ian Jukes presents an exploration of the skills and competencies that are essential in the digital age, beyond traditional literacy. The authors introduce the concept of "21st Century Fluencies," which encompasses not just the ability to read and write, but also the skills needed to navigate and thrive in a technology-rich, globalized world. The book emphasizes the importance of adapting education to include these fluencies to fully prepare students for the modern workforce and society. The authors outline strategies for educators to incorporate these skills into their teaching methods.

## Review
The book makes a significant contribution to the discussion of education in the digital age. Its emphasis on practical fluencies rather than traditional literacies offers a fresh perspective that addresses the evolving needs of learners today. One of the strengths of the book is its actionable approach—offering educators specific ways to integrate fluency skills into their curriculum. However, some critics might find that the book lacks depth in certain theoretical foundations, favoring a more pragmatic viewpoint. Overall, the book is praised for its forward-thinking approach and practical applications.

## Key Takeaways
1. **21st Century Fluencies**: The book identifies and elaborates on several critical fluencies including Solution Fluency, Information Fluency, Creativity Fluency, Media Fluency, and Collaboration Fluency.
2. **Adaptability in Education**: Educators must be flexible and proactive in updating curricula and teaching methods to ensure students develop these fluencies.
3. **Practical Integration**: The authors provide specific strategies and techniques for educators to incorporate these skills into classroom experiences.
4. **Global Competency**: Recognizing the importance of preparing students for global citizenship in a connected world.
5. **Emphasis on Problem-Solving**: Encouraging learners to develop problem-solving skills through relevant and meaningful learning experiences.

## Recommendation
"Literacy Is NOT Enough" is highly recommended for educators, curriculum developers, and education policy makers who are focused on modernizing classroom experiences to better prepare students for the challenges of the 21st century. It is especially beneficial for those seeking practical and innovative ways to integrate technology and critical thinking skills into education.
