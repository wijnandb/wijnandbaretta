# How I Raised Myself From Failure To Success In Selling

## Summary
"How I Raised Myself From Failure To Success In Selling" by Frank Bettger is a motivational and instructional guide for individuals involved in sales or any occupation that involves influencing people. The book is part autobiography and part self-help, where Bettger shares personal anecdotes from his own journey from a failed insurance salesman to a successful figure in the sales industry. Bettger emphasizes the importance of enthusiasm, planning, understanding customer needs, handling rejections, and maintaining a positive mindset, among other principles. The guidance is encapsulated in real-life examples, making the advice practical and relatable to aspiring and practicing sales professionals alike.

## Review
Frank Bettger's book is often praised for its straightforward and relatable writing style. The strength of the book lies in its practical advice, drawn from Bettger's personal experiences. Readers appreciate the way Bettger blends storytelling with actionable insights, making the strategies easy to understand and implement. Some critiques point to the dated nature of certain references, as the book was originally published in the mid-20th century. However, many of the core principles regarding human behavior and relationship-building remain relevant. The book's impact endures, serving as a staple recommendation in many sales training programs.

## Key Takeaways
- **Enthusiasm is Key**: Approaching tasks with genuine enthusiasm can significantly impact one's success in sales.
- **Understanding Clients**: Listening to and understanding the needs of clients is crucial to uncovering sales opportunities.
- **Persistence and Resilience**: Persistence in the face of rejection is essential for long-term success.
- **Preparation and Planning**: Thorough preparation and strategic planning lead to better performance and results.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Continuously learning and refining one's skills is important for professional growth.

## Recommendation
"How I Raised Myself From Failure To Success In Selling" is highly recommended for salespeople, entrepreneurs, and anyone involved in client-facing roles. Its timeless advice on building relationships and improving sales techniques can benefit beginners and seasoned professionals alike. The book's blend of autobiographical storytelling and practical advice makes it an engaging and valuable read for those looking to enhance their selling skills and achieve success in their professional lives.
