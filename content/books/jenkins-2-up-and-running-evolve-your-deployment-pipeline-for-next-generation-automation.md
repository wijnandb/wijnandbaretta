# Jenkins 2: Up and Running: Evolve Your Deployment Pipeline for Next Generation Automation

## Summary
"Jenkins 2: Up and Running" by Brent Laster is a comprehensive guide focused on the upgraded features and capabilities of Jenkins 2, emphasizing its role in automating deployment pipelines. The book covers the enhanced functionalities provided by Jenkins 2, including the integration of the Pipeline plugin, which facilitates a more advanced and streamlined way to manage CI/CD processes. It addresses setting up Jenkins, configuring pipelines using both code and graphical interfaces, and integrating Jenkins with other tools and platforms for efficient automation and continuous delivery.

## Review
The book is well-received for its practical approach and detailed explanations, making it a valuable resource for both beginners and experienced Jenkins users looking to upgrade their knowledge for Jenkins 2. One notable strength of the book is its hands-on tutorials and examples, which allow readers to apply concepts in real-world scenarios. However, some readers may find the content dense, particularly those new to CI/CD concepts, as it covers a wide range of advanced topics. Nonetheless, the book is praised for its clarity and ability to provide a thorough understanding of Jenkins as a modern automation tool.

## Key Takeaways
- **Pipeline as Code**: Learn how to implement Jenkins Pipelines using code, allowing for version control and easier collaboration.
- **Enhanced Automation**: Understand how Jenkins 2 provides enhanced automation capabilities, making complex deployments more manageable.
- **Integration**: Discover how to seamlessly integrate Jenkins with other tools and services to create a robust CI/CD pipeline.
- **Best Practices**: Gain insights into best practices for designing and maintaining efficient and scalable deployment pipelines.

## Recommendation
"Jenkins 2: Up and Running" is highly recommended for DevOps engineers, system administrators, and software developers looking to transition from Jenkins 1 to Jenkins 2 or enhance their current CI/CD pipeline practices. The book is particularly beneficial for those interested in leveraging the full potential of Jenkins to achieve next-generation automation in their deployment processes. With its clear guidance and practical examples, it serves as an essential resource for mastering Jenkins 2.
