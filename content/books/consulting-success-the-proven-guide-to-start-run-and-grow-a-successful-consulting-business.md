# Consulting Success: The Proven Guide to Start, Run and Grow a Successful Consulting Business

## Summary
"Consulting Success: The Proven Guide to Start, Run and Grow a Successful Consulting Business" by Michael Zipursky is a comprehensive guide designed for aspiring and established consultants. The book provides practical advice on launching, managing, and expanding a successful consulting practice. It delves into strategies for gaining clients, optimizing business operations, and sustaining growth in a competitive market. Zipursky draws from his vast experience in consulting, offering insights on client acquisition, pricing strategies, and the importance of building strong professional relationships. The book also tackles common challenges faced by consultants and offers solutions and tactics to overcome them.

## Review
Michael Zipursky’s book is a well-organized and insightful resource for those in the consulting industry. Its main strength lies in its actionable advice and real-world applications. Zipursky's writing is engaging, and the content is logically structured, making complex business concepts accessible to a broad audience. One notable critique, however, could be the book's focus on generalist strategies, which may not address specific niche consulting fields in detail. Nevertheless, the book’s pragmatic approach and emphasis on real-life examples make it a valuable read for anyone looking to improve their consulting business.

## Key Takeaways
- **Client Acquisition**: Emphasizes the importance of understanding the client's needs and developing tailored solutions to secure long-term engagements.
- **Pricing Strategies**: Offers insights into setting appropriate pricing structures that reflect value while remaining competitive.
- **Relationship Building**: Highlights the role of strong relationships and networking in sustaining and growing a consulting business.
- **Business Operations**: Discusses the importance of streamlining operations to maximize efficiency and scalability.
- **Problem-solving**: Provides strategies for common consulting challenges and ways to overcome potential barriers to success.

## Recommendation
Anyone interested in or currently working within the consulting industry would benefit from reading this book. Entrepreneurs, business consultants, and those considering a career change into consulting will find its insights particularly useful. Michael Zipursky’s book is recommended for its practical guidance and clear strategies that can be applied to both new and existing consulting practices.
