# Reality Is Broken: Why Games Make Us Better and How They Can Change the World

## Summary
"Reality Is Broken: Why Games Make Us Better and How They Can Change the World" by Jane McGonigal explores the potential of video games to improve our lives and transform the world. McGonigal argues that the skills and emotional resilience developed through gaming can be harnessed for real-world problem-solving and personal fulfillment. The book is structured around the idea that many aspects of reality are lacking in the same kind of compelling engagement and satisfaction that games provide. McGonigal outlines various ways in which game mechanics can be applied to education, business, and social issues to enhance motivation and cooperation.

## Review
Jane McGonigal’s "Reality Is Broken" provides a compelling argument for the value of games beyond entertainment. The book is praised for its thorough research and engaging writing style, which makes complex ideas accessible to a broad audience. One of its strengths is the author's ability to illustrate her ideas with concrete examples of games that have successfully contributed to real-world positive impacts. However, some critics might argue that McGonigal is overly optimistic and may not fully account for the negative aspects of gaming addiction or the socio-economic barriers to game-based solutions. Despite this, the book is considered thought-provoking and influential in game studies and beyond.

## Key Takeaways
- **Engagement**: Games offer a sense of purpose, achievement, and happiness that can be replicated in real-life settings.
- **Skill Development**: Video games help players develop problem-solving skills, strategic thinking, and collaboration.
- **Real-World Application**: The mechanics and principles that make games enjoyable can be used to address societal and personal challenges.
- **Community and Collaboration**: Online games foster community building and collaboration, which can spill over into real-world social structures.
- **Future Potential**: Games hold the potential to provide innovative solutions for global challenges, including education, healthcare, and civic engagement.

## Recommendation
"Reality Is Broken" is recommended for gamers, educators, psychologists, and anyone interested in the intersection of technology, culture, and personal development. The book offers valuable insights into how the principles of gaming can be applied to enhance motivation, productivity, and social well-being, making it a powerful read for individuals looking to leverage the positive aspects of games for personal or societal benefit.
