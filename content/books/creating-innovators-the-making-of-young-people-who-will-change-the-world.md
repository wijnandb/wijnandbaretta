# Creating Innovators: The Making of Young People Who Will Change the World

## Summary
"Creating Innovators: The Making of Young People Who Will Change the World" by Tony Wagner explores the methods and environments that raise innovative individuals capable of addressing future challenges. The book delves into educational techniques, parental roles, and the importance of mentorship in nurturing creativity and a problem-solving mindset. Through detailed examples and stories, Wagner examines the characteristics and educational approaches that underpin the development of young innovators. He highlights the necessity for a shift from conventional rote education to more dynamic and collaborative learning experiences.

## Review
Tony Wagner's book is insightful in challenging the current educational paradigm, especially concerning how creativity and innovation can be fostered from a young age. Its strength lies in the combination of real-life narratives and actionable insights supported by thorough research. The author's focus on storytelling effectively illustrates the principles he advocates, although some readers might find the anecdotal style less structured than a traditional academic approach. Wagner's passion for transforming education to meet future demands is palpable and makes the book a compelling read for educators, parents, and policymakers.

## Key Takeaways
- **Experiential Learning**: Emphasizes the importance of hands-on, project-based learning over standard testing.
- **Encouragement of Curiosity**: Highlights fostering curiosity and questioning as critical to developing innovative thinking.
- **Role of Mentorship**: Illustrates how effective mentorship can guide young people in identifying and pursuing their passions.
- **Redefining Success**: Advocates for redefining success beyond grades and test scores to include creativity and collaborative skills.
- **Parental Influence**: Points out the vital role parents play in nurturing an environment conducive to trial-and-error and learning from failure.

## Recommendation
"Creating Innovators" is an essential read for educators, parents, and policymakers interested in cultivating a new generation of thinkers and problem solvers. The book is especially relevant for those seeking to understand and implement educational reforms that emphasize creativity and innovation. Its insights can guide anyone involved in youth development towards approaches that prepare young people to navigate and shape an unpredictable future.
