# Disaster Inc by Caimh McDonnell

## Summary
*Disaster Inc* follows Bunny McGarry, a former Dublin detective who finds himself in New York City entangled in a series of unexpected, chaotic events. What starts as a simple breakfast quickly spirals into a diner robbery that introduces Bunny to a complex conspiracy involving former government operatives and high-stakes financial schemes. Known for his wit, Bunny navigates these challenges with his characteristic humor and tenacity, making new allies and foes along the way. This action-packed novel is the first in McDonnell’s McGarry Stateside series, bringing Irish humor to the streets of America.

## Review
*Disaster Inc* delivers a mix of fast-paced action and humor that fans of McDonnell's Dublin Trilogy will appreciate. Bunny McGarry’s character is compelling and relatable, with a depth that keeps readers invested as he faces increasingly absurd yet dangerous situations. The novel’s humor and wit are balanced with suspense, and while it rewards readers familiar with McDonnell’s previous works, it stands well on its own as an entertaining and accessible read. The blend of crime fiction and comedy makes *Disaster Inc* a refreshing entry in the genre.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Character Development**: Bunny McGarry’s journey from Ireland to America highlights his adaptability, resourcefulness, and resilience in unfamiliar situations.
2. **Humor Amidst Chaos**: The novel’s humor offers relief amidst the suspense, adding a unique twist to typical crime fiction narratives.
3. **Standalone Appeal**: While it’s part of a series, *Disaster Inc* functions as a standalone novel, offering a complete and engaging narrative.
4. **New Setting, Same Charm**: The shift to a U.S. setting adds fresh challenges for Bunny, while McDonnell retains the Irish charm and wit that define his style.
5. **Friendship and Loyalty**: Bunny’s alliances and loyalty to friends are central themes, emphasizing the importance of trust and camaraderie in his chaotic journey.

*Disaster Inc* is recommended for fans of crime fiction who enjoy a comedic touch, as well as readers new to McDonnell’s work seeking a unique blend of action and Irish wit.
