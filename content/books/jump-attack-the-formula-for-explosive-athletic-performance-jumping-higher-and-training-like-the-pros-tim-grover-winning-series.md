# Jump Attack: The Formula for Explosive Athletic Performance, Jumping Higher, and Training Like the Pros

## Summary
"Jump Attack" by Tim S. Grover is a comprehensive guide focused on improving athletic performance with a strong emphasis on developing explosive power, particularly for jumping higher. The book is part of the Tim Grover Winning Series and draws on Grover's extensive experience working with elite athletes like Michael Jordan, Kobe Bryant, and Dwyane Wade. Grover presents a structured and progressive 12-week workout program that includes exercises, techniques, and drills aimed at increasing vertical leap, speed, strength, and agility. It emphasizes the role of discipline, mental toughness, and tailored training routines to reach peak athletic potential.

## Review
"Jump Attack" has been well-received for its practical approach and the credibility that Grover brings as a renowned trainer for some of the most successful basketball players in history. The book's strengths lie in its clear, step-by-step program and its focus on both physical and mental preparation. However, some critiques suggest that the regimen may be too intense for beginners or those not already conditioned for high levels of athletic training. Despite this, it remains a valuable resource for athletes looking to gain an edge in their performance.

## Key Takeaways
- **Structured Regimen**: The 12-week program is designed to systematically build explosive power, improve jumping ability, and enhance overall athletic performance.
- **Mental Toughness**: Success in sports is not just physical but also mental; discipline and a strong mindset are crucial.
- **Professional Insights**: The book offers insights and routines used by top-tier athletes, distilled into actionable advice for readers.
- **Versatile Adaptation**: While geared toward basketball players, the principles can benefit any explosive athletic performance.

## Recommendation
"Jump Attack" is highly recommended for serious athletes, particularly those in sports like basketball, volleyball, or track and field, who are committed to enhancing their explosive power and overall athletic performance. It is also beneficial for trainers and coaches seeking structured programs backed by successful case studies. Beginners or casual athletes should approach with caution or consider starting with modified versions of the workouts provided.
