# Overview of "Hiring for Attitude: A Revolutionary Approach to Recruiting and Selecting People with Both Tremendous Skills and Superb Attitude" by Mark Murphy

## Summary
"Hiring for Attitude" by Mark Murphy is a guide that shifts the focus of recruitment from skills-based hiring to prioritizing the attitude of the candidates. Murphy argues that skills can be taught, but attitude forms the core of how an employee will fit within the company culture and contribute to its success. Through a mixture of research-backed insights and practical tools, the book provides strategies for identifying the right attitudes during the hiring process. It also delves into techniques for framing interview questions and creating an organizational culture that fosters positive attitudes.

## Review
The book has been praised for its innovative approach and clarity in addressing a common but often misunderstood aspect of hiring. Its strength lies in Murphy's ability to back up claims with substantial research and real-world examples. However, some critiques highlight that the emphasis on attitude may overlook the importance of specific skill sets needed for particular roles. Despite this, "Hiring for Attitude" is often regarded as a valuable resource for HR professionals and managers who aim to enhance their hiring practices by incorporating cultural fit as a central component.

## Key Takeaways
- **Culture Fit Over Skills**: Focus on hiring individuals whose attitudes align with the company's values and culture, since skills can be more easily developed.
- **Behavioral Interview Techniques**: Implement interview techniques that draw out a candidate's natural attitudes rather than rehearsed responses.
- **Red Flags**: Identify and understand potential attitude red flags that might predict future difficulties in team integration and performance.
- **Create the Right Culture**: Foster a company culture that emphasizes and rewards the right attitudes to maintain alignment with the organizational goals.
- **Use Research-Based Strategies**: Utilize data-driven approaches to enhance the recruitment process, making it more effective in identifying the best candidates.

## Recommendation
"Hiring for Attitude" is particularly beneficial for HR professionals, hiring managers, and organizational leaders looking to refine their recruitment processes and build stronger, more cohesive teams. Those interested in organizational development and improving workplace culture might also find the book's insights valuable, as it provides actionable steps to align talent acquisition with broader business goals.
