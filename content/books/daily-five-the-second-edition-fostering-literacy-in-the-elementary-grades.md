# Daily Five, The (Second Edition): Fostering Literacy in the Elementary Grades

**Authors**: Gail Boushey and Joan Moser

## Summary:
The second edition of "The Daily Five" by Gail Boushey and Joan Moser offers a structured framework for fostering literacy in elementary school students. This educational method emphasizes student independence and choice in reading and writing activities. The book outlines five core components, known as the "Daily Five": Read to Self, Work on Writing, Read to Someone, Listen to Reading, and Word Work. The focus is on creating a classroom environment where students are engaged, take responsibility for their learning, and develop literacy skills in a meaningful context. The authors provide practical strategies for implementing the Daily Five, addressing classroom management, time management, and adapting the framework to fit the needs of diverse learners.

## Review:
"The Daily Five" is widely regarded for its clear and practical approach to literacy instruction. Boushey and Moser's framework helps educators create a classroom culture that promotes literacy and builds student independence. A notable strength of the book is its step-by-step guidance and the inclusion of real-world classroom examples. However, some educators may find the prescribed structure too rigid for certain student populations, requiring adaptation to meet specific learning needs. Overall, the book has made a significant impact on literacy instruction practices and continues to be a valuable resource for teachers.

## Key Takeaways:
1. **Student Choice and Independence**: Encouraging students to make choices about their literacy activities fosters engagement and independence.
2. **Daily Routine**: Establishing a consistent routine with the Daily Five helps students become self-directed learners.
3. **Classroom Management**: Effective introduction and management of the Daily Five components are crucial for successful implementation.
4. **Differentiation**: The framework can be adapted to cater to the diverse literacy needs of students.
5. **Assessment**: Ongoing assessment and observation are vital to guide instruction and student progress.

## Recommendation:
"The Daily Five, Second Edition" is highly recommended for elementary educators seeking to enhance their literacy instruction practices. Teachers looking to create a student-centered learning environment that values choice and independence will benefit greatly from its insights and strategies. The book's practical examples and adaptable framework make it a resource suitable for both new and experienced teachers aiming to foster a love of literacy among their students.
