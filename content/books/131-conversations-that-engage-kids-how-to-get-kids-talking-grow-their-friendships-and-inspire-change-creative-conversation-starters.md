# 131 Conversations That Engage Kids: How to Get Kids Talking, Grow Their Friendships, and Inspire Change (Creative Conversation Starters)

## Summary
A Simple And Easy Guide To Help Improve Communication Skills,Get Rid of Social Anxiety And Thrive In Social Situations! All conversations start small. In most cases, it starts with a simple greeting, and sometimes ends with one, too. The greeting part is easy though - the challenge is in how to make small talks end big. Making your conversation grow can help you a lot in your business, relationships and work. It can also create new friendships and create opportunities to advance in your job. Some say, you need to have a lot of charisma to make small talks grow into memorable conversations. If you do not have it, your conversation is probably going to fall flat. That is completely false. Charisma only helps you converse easily with other people. It is only the sugar coating. What makes the conversation grow is your ability to input substance into the topic. You can do this by stocking up on knowledge, listening, using body language appropriately, and developing your coping ability. This book will teach you the proven steps and strategies in making your conversation grow, dealing with the different kinds of conversationalists, and ending your conversation while leaving the other party interested in talking to you again.
