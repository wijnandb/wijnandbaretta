# Technology, Learning, and the AI-Driven Life

Technology has always been a driving force behind progress, but in recent years, artificial intelligence has taken center stage. We're entering a new era—one where AI isn't just changing industries; it's changing how we think, create, learn, and live.

For me, this site is more than a collection of thoughts on tech and education. It's become a personal and professional exploration of how we can use AI to lead more **fulfilled, productive, and creative lives**—and help others do the same.

## A New Focus: AI as a Life Multiplier

Lately, I've been asking myself: _What if AI could be more than a tool? What if it could become a partner in designing a better life?_
Not just in business or productivity—but in how we learn, create, connect, and grow.

That's the journey I'm on now:
- **Using AI to build products faster**
- **Automating what drains energy, so I can focus on what gives energy**
- **Creating personal workflows and systems to stay on track (especially with ADHD)**
- **Helping others harness AI to multiply their own ideas and impact**

## What You'll Find Here

This site is a mix of thoughts, projects, experiments, and reflections. It's shaped by my experiences—past and present—as a startup founder, builder, educator, and now, an AI-augmented human. Key themes include:

- **AI and Personal Fulfillment**: How I'm using AI to design a happier, more intentional life—and how you can too.
- **Education and Impact**: Tech-enabled ways to make learning more meaningful for kids, especially through projects like after-school programs.
- **Entrepreneurship and Startups**: Building things from scratch, validating ideas, and scaling smart—faster and more sustainably with AI.
- **Digital Tools and Automation**: Making tech work *for* you—whether you're an individual, a small team, or an organization.
- **Working with ADHD**: Tools, strategies, and systems that actually help creative brains stay on course.

## Why Connect?

## My Mission

I believe **technology is most powerful when it empowers people**—when it frees us up to do what matters most. My mission is to **use AI not just to do more, but to live better**—and to help others do the same.

Whether it's through hands-on support, strategic advice, speaking, or software, I'm here to make ideas real—and make life better along the way.
