Youth Venture Studio#

Build real things. Learn real skills. Find your path.#

Are you between 16 and 28 and want to learn skills that actually matter?
Do you want to build real projects, work with AI, and join a community of ambitious young makers?

The Youth Venture Studio is a guided program where you learn by doing — with mentorship, peers, and AI tools that help you build faster than you ever thought possible.

This is not school.
This is not a course.
This is your launchpad.

Why Join?#

1. Build Real Projects#

No homework.
No exams.
You build things that actually work.

Apps, tools, websites, automations, MVPs — whatever you want to create, we help you ship it.

2. Learn Skills When You Need Them (Just-in-Time Learning)#

You learn exactly what you need, when you need it:

  • Design a landing page? Learn branding today.
  • Build a feature? Learn Django or Next.js today.
  • Talk to users? Learn interviewing this week.
  • Want to automate something? Learn AI prompting now.

Learning becomes fast, relevant, and fun.

3. Join a Community of Young Builders#

Meet people who are:

  • curious
  • ambitious
  • creative
  • motivated
  • ready to build things together

No more trying to do everything alone.

4. Work With AI to Build Faster#

You’ll learn how to:

  • use AI as a coding assistant
  • generate ideas
  • automate work
  • build prototypes quickly
  • validate concepts
  • design smarter workflows

AI becomes your superpower.

5. Discover Your Purpose by Doing#

Not through tests or theory, but through:

  • creating
  • trying
  • failing
  • succeeding
  • solving real problems
  • helping real people

You’ll find what energizes you — and what doesn’t.

Who Is This For?#

Young people (16–28) who:

  • want to build something meaningful
  • are curious about entrepreneurship or tech
  • feel bored or stuck in traditional education
  • want to learn faster
  • want to be part of a tribe
  • want to find direction
  • want to grow in confidence
  • want to prepare for the real world

No experience needed.
Just curiosity.

Who This Is Not For#

  • People looking for a traditional classroom
  • People who want guaranteed income next week
  • People who aren’t willing to try, build, or collaborate
  • People who only want to watch and never make

This is a place for makers, not spectators.

What You’ll Get#

1. Weekly Sessions#

Hands-on building sessions, feedback, and guidance.

2. Mentorship & Coaching#

Help with:

  • choosing projects
  • overcoming roadblocks
  • learning new tools
  • dealing with failure
  • working with AI
  • finding your strengths

3. Access to Tools & Templates#

  • AI workflows
  • coding templates
  • MVP frameworks
  • landing page builders
  • idea validation scripts

4. A Portfolio You Can Show to Anyone#

By the end, you’ll have:

  • at least one deployed project
  • a public portfolio
  • a story you can tell employers and clients
  • confidence from actually building things

5. A Tribe#

Most importantly:
You won’t be alone.

What You Will Learn#

  • Entrepreneurship (the real kind)
  • AI-assisted building
  • Coding fundamentals
  • Automation
  • UI/UX basics
  • Storytelling & pitching
  • Idea validation
  • Working in teams
  • Focus & execution
  • Self-motivation
  • Confidence through competence

You will learn the way entrepreneurs learn:
just-in-time, not just-in-case.

How It Works#

  1. Apply (short form)
  2. Interview (we want good fit, not perfect skills)
  3. Join a cohort (4–8–12 weeks)
  4. Pick a project
  5. Learn what you need, when you need it
  6. Build, test, improve
  7. Launch
  8. Demo Day
  9. Join community as alumni

Every week, you make progress.
Every week, you become more capable.

Why It Works#

Because it’s built on science:

  • Autonomy → motivation
  • Competence → confidence
  • Relatedness → belonging
  • Meaning → purpose
  • Just-in-time learning → retention and excitement
  • AI → speed and empowerment

The Youth Venture Studio is happiness-by-design.

Cost#

We believe this should be accessible.

We offer:

  • Free participation for selected applicants,
    OR
  • Low-cost membership,
    PLUS
  • Scholarships for anyone who needs them.

Money should never be the reason you don’t join.

Apply Now#

If you want to:

  • build
  • learn
  • grow
  • meet your people
  • discover your potential
  • and shape your future

then apply today.

We’ll help you with everything else.
