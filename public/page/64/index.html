Introduction to Graph Theory (Dover Books on Mathematics)

Introduction to Graph Theory (Dover Books on Mathematics) Summary This book contains research articles and extended abstracts submitted by participants in the Planar Graphs Workshop held at DIMACS in November 1991, one of four workshops held during the DIMACS Special Year on Graph Theory and Algorithms. With more than seventy participants, the workshop drew many of the top experts in this area. The book covers a wide range of topics, including enumeration, characterization problems, algorithms, extremal problems, and network flows and geometry.
