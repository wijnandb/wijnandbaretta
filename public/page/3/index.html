Mindset: The New Psychology of Success by Carol S. Dweck Summary “Mindset: The New Psychology of Success” by Carol S. Dweck introduces two primary mindsets: the fixed mindset and the growth mindset. A fixed mindset is the belief that abilities are static and unchangeable, leading to a desire to appear smart and an aversion to failure. Conversely, a growth mindset embraces challenges and sees failures as opportunities to develop and improve abilities.
